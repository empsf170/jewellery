Our Story

Founded in 1920, Aura Luxury began as a small boutique in the heart of Paris. Today, we are recognized globally for our commitment to exquisite craftsmanship and unparalleled elegance.

We source only the finest materials, from ethically mined diamonds to the highest grade precious metals, ensuring each piece is a masterpiece.
